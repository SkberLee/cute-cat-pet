Source: https://vuena.itch.io/blocky-pets-free
Archive used: Cute-Pets-Wildlife-FREE-Sample-CC0.zip (v3 designer-toy sample)
In-game file: assets/models/cute_cat_rigged.glb
  - Derived from GLB/animal_cat.glb
  - Albedo resized to 1024² for mobile; LOD1 mesh node stripped so only LOD0 draws
  - Polycount: ~7,188 triangles (LOD0). Original pack also includes LOD1 (~2,452 tris).

Animations (skeletal, in-place): idle, idle_alt, walk, run, jump, eat, sit, sleep,
attack, call, hit, death.

Mapping in 治愈猫:
  sit   -> default room idle loop
  call  -> 摸摸 (pet)
  eat   -> 喂食 (feed)
  jump  -> 逗逗 (play / soft pounce)
